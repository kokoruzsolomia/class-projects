Just Tornadoes 1 (JT1) is a popular Roblox storm-chasing game created by the developer PaperToPaper2 that focuses on simplicity and lightweight performance. Key Features • Low-End Optimized: It is built specifically to run smoothly on older mobile devices, low-end PCs, and weaker hardware that typically lags during heavy Roblox graphics. • Tornado Variety: Players can encounter, track, and intercept unpredictable tornadoes ranging all the way up to severe, massive EF6-strength storms. • Vehicle Fleet: The game features a selection of customizable or pre-built storm-intercept vehicles (SIVs) used to drive directly into or near the path of the tornadoes. JT1 vs. Just Tornadoes 2 (JT2) • JT1: Features a more basic, classic visual design to keep processing demands minimal. • JT2: Serves as a higher-fidelity sequel with enhanced graphics, improved performance scripts, and deeper atmospheric physics for players with stronger devices. Helicity

Helicity is a popular, physics-based semi-realistic storm-chasing game on Roblox. Unlike lightweight games, it features a more detailed simulation of atmospheric physics and severe weather. Core Gameplay & Objectives • Data Gathering: Your main objective is to track storms and collect valuable data. You do this by scanning mobile radars, deploying weather stations (like Mesonets), and dropping probes. • Tornado Interception: You can drive specialized Tornado Intercept Vehicles (T.I.V.) directly into a tornado's path—a mechanic known as "intercepting"—to measure extreme wind speeds. • Realistic Weather Systems: The game simulates a full supercell structure, including hook echoes on radar, hail, lightning, the Forward Flank Downdraft (FFD), and Rear Flank Downdraft (RFD). • Thermodynamics Engine: Storm behavior is influenced by live environment stats like CAPE (Convective Available Potential Energy) and wind shear, meaning some storms will spawn massive, violent tornadoes while others fizzle out. Quick Tips for Beginners 1. Best Spawn Location: When you start, spawn in Mica. It is located right in the dead center of the map, making it the most practical starting point to reach storms no matter where they spawn. 2. Read the Radar: Open your UI and look for a classic "hook echo" shape on the radar reflectivity map. This tells you exactly where a tornado is forming or tracking. 3. Safety First: If you are driving a standard vehicle (like the starter Ford Bronco), stay on the outer edges of the storm. You need heavy armored interceptors (like the Titus or Dominator) to drive into a tornado without getting lofted into the air. Storm Chasers Reborn 7

Storm Chasers Reborn 7 [2.1] is a popular, simulation-focused Roblox Storm Chasers Reborn 7 experience that emphasizes scientific data collection and realistic tornado mechanics over arcade-style play. Core Gameplay Loop • Scientific Data & Photography: You chase severe supercell storms to gather scientific metrics, study atmospheric soundings (temperature and wind profiles), and snap photos of forming twisters to earn cash. • Vehicle Upgrades: You use earned money to buy faster, heavier, and more durable vehicles—including specialized intercept rigs—capable of withstanding intense winds and flying debris. • Radar & Weather Systems: The game features an advanced radar system with velocity, reflectivity (including hook echoes), and correlation coefficients to help you track storm movement. Key Mechanics & Challenges • Resource Management: You have to monitor your fuel supply and plan your route carefully—forgetting to fill up at a gas station can leave you stranded. • Night Chasing Risks: Chasing at night dramatically increases difficulty because visibility drops to near-zero in rural areas, making radar interpretation and situational awareness critical. • Hardware Requirements: Unlike lighter games built for phones, Storm Chasers Reborn 7 features heavy environmental details and is best played on a PC rather than mobile devices. Severity

Severity is an in-development, high-fidelity Roblox storm-chasing simulator created by developers Willzuh (builder) and Siryzm (programmer). Core Gameplay & Features • Setting & Map: Set across the rolling prairies and towns of the fictional state of Keysota, featuring detailed road networks, wind farms, and regional hubs. • Hyper-Realistic Tornadoes & Physics: Built with advanced debris, lofting physics, and intense wind-proximity effects where getting closer to the funnel increases vehicle-damage risk and pushing force. • Scientific Tools & Probes: Players can open trunk interfaces to deploy data-collecting probes into active tornado paths to read pressure drops and metrics. • Vehicles & Customization: Features standard civilian vehicles, modification shops (for paint, tires, and mechanical repairs), and heavy-duty Tornado Intercept Vehicles (TIVs) like TIV-2 and Doghouse. • Radar & Meteorology UI: Includes radar technology, distance-measuring tools, and environmental tracking (such as skew-T diagrams and SPC outlooks). System Requirements & Status • Hardware Warning: Unlike lightweight mobile-friendly games, Severity requires a high-end device to handle its complex particle, lighting, and destruction physics smoothly. • Maturity Rating: Rated 16+ on Roblox. • Current Status: In active beta/development with ongoing public tests and community sneak-peeks. Tornado Simulator 2

Tornado Simulator 2 is a classic, legacy Roblox storm-chasing and town-survival game created by Alex_ander. Core Gameplay & Teams • Selectable Teams: Players can choose to play as Storm Chasers, Citizens, or Police, each with different community roles and vehicle access. • Weather Lifecycle: Storms start with a severe thunderstorm warning, turn into a tornado watch, and eventually drop a live funnel cloud onto towns like Wakefield, Rockford, or Andover. When a tornado touches down, local sirens ring out for 60 seconds. • Town Destruction: Tornadoes (ranging from standard ropes to massive multi-vortex wedge shapes) tear through buildings, cut power lines, and knock down trees, leaving behind real-time debris and power outages. Available Vehicles • Scout Pickup (Free): An XP2 pickup truck equipped with hail-proof window covers. • Scout Sedan (Free): An X1 sedan with reinforced glass for basic tracking. • TIV - Tornado Intercept Vehicle (Free): A civilian SUV fitted with hydraulic anchor spikes so you can lock yourself to the ground and survive high winds. • Scout SUV / TIV 2 (Paid/Group): Upgraded vehicles featuring built-in mesonet equipment or group-exclusive body styles like the Dominator. Important Notes • Not for Mobile: The game was originally built for desktop/PC and is explicitly not designed to work properly on mobile devices. • Legacy Status: The original game is mostly preserved as a classic title and is no longer receiving active content updates.
