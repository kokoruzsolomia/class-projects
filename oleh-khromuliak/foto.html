It is officially recognized as the widest tornado ever recorded in history. Core Specifications • Maximum Width: 2.6 miles (4.2 km) at its peak. • Peak Wind Speeds: Radar measurements (from the RaXPol mobile doppler radar) clocked internal subvortices spinning at up to 313 mph (504 km/h). reed timer

Dr. Reed Timmer is an American extreme meteorologist and storm chaser born on March 17, 1980, who is widely known for driving heavy armored vehicles directly inside active tornadoes. Background & Education • Academic Credentials: Earned a PhD in Meteorology from the University of Oklahoma. • Origin of Passion: Began chasing storms after his childhood home in Grand Rapids, Michigan, was pelted by large hail when he was 13 years old. • Media Stardom: Rose to fame as a central star of the Discovery Channel reality series Storm Chasers, as well as digital shows like Tornado Chasers and live streaming projects. The "Dominator" Fleet Timmer is famous for designing and building specialized, heavily modified SRV Dominator (Storm Research Vehicle) armored SUVs to survive inside violent vortexes: • Design Features: They feature custom-molded aerodynamic polycarbonate bodies, reinforced roll cages, bulletproof glass, hydraulic suspension systems, and spike anchors to hug the ground and keep winds from getting underneath. • Science & Sensors: Instead of just watching storms, Timmer uses air cannons and small rockets to shoot scientific probes and sensors directly into the walls and base of tornadoes. • Active Rig: He primarily operates Dominator 3 (and secondary chase vehicles like Dominator Fore) equipped with Starlink for live-streaming and data transmission from the heart of supercells. Real-World Career Highlights • Over 1,000 Tornadoes: He has intercepted and documented more than 1,000 tornadoes and roughly 50 hurricanes over a 30+ year field career. • Inspiration for Twisters: His real-life extreme probing, rocket-deploying tactics, and high-adrenaline chasing style heavily inspired the science and characters in the Hollywood movie Twisters (2024), where he also contributed behind the scenes. Dominator 3 is a famous armored storm chasing vehicle created by the well-known American meteorologist and extreme weather researcher Reed Timmer. This massive 7-ton interceptor is custom-built to drive straight into the core of violent tornadoes to collect crucial scientific data. Key Features of the Vehicle: • Base Platform: The machine is built on a heavy-duty 2012 Ford F-350 Super Duty chassis. This is the first interceptor in Timmer's lineup not built on a General Motors platform. • Armor and Protection: The body is completely rebuilt using high-strength steel, Kevlar, and high-density polyethylene. The windows and lights are protected by double-layered polycarbonate (Lexan). • Wind Aerodynamics: It features a hydraulic suspension system that allows the vehicle to literally drop to the ground before a strike so the wind cannot get underneath and flip it. Hydraulic ground spikes are also mounted on the sides to anchor the truck into the dirt. • Scientific Equipment: A rocket-launching system is mounted on the roof to shoot special meteorological sensor rockets into the tornado to measure pressure, humidity, and wind speed. • Engine (ENG): Under the hood lies a heavy-duty 6.7-liter PowerStroke V8 turbodiesel engine putting out 400 horsepower and 800 lb-ft of torque. It was purposefully kept mostly stock for maximum reliability in rural areas, though it recently received a complete overhaul and refresh to extend its lifespan. • Cost: The total cost to build and equip this extreme machine is estimated at around $750,000. TORNADO ATTAK

The Tornado Attack Vehicle (TA-1) is a highly modified, armored vehicle designed specifically to drive directly into tornadoes to gather scientific data. Key Specifications & History • Origin & Chassis: It was originally built on a custom Jeep chassis intended for Baja racing before being heavily re-engineered for storm chasing. • Creator & Owner: Built and operated by storm chaser Steve Green in the early 2000s (around 2002–2004). • Power & Engine: Equipped with a high-performance, 700-horsepower V8 engine. • Armor & Protection: Features bulletproof Lexan polycarbonate windows capable of stopping a .38 caliber round, an aerodynamic low profile, and thick steel body armor (with over $500,000 invested in development). • eBay Sale: In May 2011, the vehicle was listed for sale on eBay with a starting bid of $75,000 and a "Buy It Now" price of $100,000. • Pop Culture: It gained fame through appearances on the Discovery Channel's Storm Chasers and later inspired numerous mods in vehicle simulation games like BeamNG.drive. It is often compared to similar heavy-duty storm-intercept vehicles, such as Sean Casey's TIV (Tornado Intercept Vehicle) and Reed Timmer's Dominator series.
